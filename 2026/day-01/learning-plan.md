💡 What is my understanding of DevOps & Cloud Engineering?

To me, DevOps is more than just tools like Docker, Kubernetes, Jenkins, or Terraform. It's a culture of collaboration, automation, continuous learning, and delivering software faster and more reliably.

Cloud Engineering is about building scalable, secure, and highly available infrastructure that enables businesses to innovate without worrying about the underlying systems. It combines infrastructure, automation, security, monitoring, and cost optimization to create resilient platforms.
